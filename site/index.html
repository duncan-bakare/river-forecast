Clonmel river level forecast

How much to trust these numbers

  • The 90% intervals are too narrow in floods: in the eight biggest past floods they contained the observed level in only about 65 to 80% of hours (see the track-record column).
  • From about 24 hours ahead, the model is little or no better than assuming the level stays where it is, because it has no rainfall forecast. At 48 hours it is not reliably better in floods.
  • It uses observed rain only and cannot see a storm that has not yet reached the rain gauges.
  • The grey and amber dashed levels are OPW statistical levels (reached 1% of the time, and the median annual maximum). They are not flood-warning levels and say nothing about flooding in Clonmel.
